## 2025-05-18 - In-memory BytesIO chart rendering for ReportLab PDFs
**Learning:** Writing Matplotlib figures to temporary image files on disk (`plt.savefig()`) and subsequently reading them in ReportLab flowables creates unnecessary filesystem I/O latency and requires explicit cleanup (`os.remove()`). ReportLab's `Image` flowable accepts standard file-like byte streams (such as `io.BytesIO`). Passing an in-memory `io.BytesIO` buffer directly to ReportLab eliminates intermediate disk write/read cycles, avoiding disk access bottlenecks and eliminating risk of leftover temp files in concurrent/containerized environments.
**Action:** When integrating Matplotlib-generated visualizations into ReportLab documents, render the plot directly into `io.BytesIO` and pass the buffer to `Image()`.
